Prices only is the Refresh prices button: live quotes for what you hold or watch (positions, real holdings, pending orders, alerts, open calls, watchlist), then fills, marks and alerts. It takes seconds. Full refresh is the other button: it scans the whole universe (prices plus a rotating slice of fundamentals), then fetches news, analyst actions and earnings for the shortlist and re-screens it. It takes one to two hours, and if the AI analyst is used in the screen, it makes one model call per shortlisted stock.

Jobs never overlap. If a timer fires while another job is running (the other timer, the nightly job or a button press), it waits, checks again every {{ auto_poll_seconds }} seconds, and starts as soon as the other job finishes. A timer that is already waiting doesn't queue extra copies, a market-hours-only run that waits past the close is dropped, and a run that waits more than {{ auto_max_wait_hours }} hours gives up its turn. Market hours = Mon–Fri 09:30–16:15 New York time (holidays not excluded). The nightly job ({{ settings.nightly_cron }} UTC) runs on its own schedule and waits the same way.
